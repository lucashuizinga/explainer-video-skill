# Brief

Fill this in before writing the script. It is the plan every later step is checked against.

- **Topic:** what the video explains, in one sentence.
- **Audience:** who watches, and what they already know.
- **The one message:** the sentence the viewer should be able to repeat afterwards.
- **What the viewer should do next:** one action, or "nothing, just understand".
- **Length:** target in seconds (default 60 to 120).
- **Language and tone:** for example Dutch, warm and plain.
- **Brand:** company, website, logo file, colours, typefaces (they go in the brand block of index.html).
- **Source material:** the documents, pages or notes every fact comes from.
- **Must include / must avoid:** terms, claims, sensitivities.
